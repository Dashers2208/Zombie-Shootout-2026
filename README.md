# Zombie Shootout 2026 – Schedule

Game schedule for the 2026 Zombie Shootout lacrosse tournament (22 teams, 33 games, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM).

**Live page:** https://Dashers2208.github.io/zombie-shootout/

## For families
- Choose your team from the dropdown, or tap any team name, to highlight all of their games.
- Use "Share team link" to send a team's schedule to relatives.
- Fields: Grass (9:00–11:20 games), Stadium (11:30 on), Turf I, Turf II.

## Updating the schedule
1. Open `index.html` in this repository and tap the pencil (Edit) icon.
2. Change the team names in the `games` list near the bottom of the file. Each entry is `[time slot, field, team A, team B, "V" or "JV"]`.
3. Commit the change. The live page updates in a minute or two.

The schedule is also written directly into the page's HTML, so edit both places or ask for a regenerated file.

Schedule subject to change by tournament staff.
